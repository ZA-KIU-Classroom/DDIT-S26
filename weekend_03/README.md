# Weekend 3 Module

## Dates
Saturday 23 May 2026
Sunday 24 May 2026
10:00 to 17:00 both days

## Theme
Midterm presentations, operating model transformation, and transformation roadmap design

## Learning objectives

By the end of Weekend 3, students should be able to:
- present and defend a midterm strategic recommendation under boardroom conditions,
- explain the difference between a traditional and an AI-first operating model,
- apply the three operating model transformation levers to a real client industry,
- sequence AI infrastructure investments using the Rogers prerequisite framework,
- analyse transformation failure patterns using the Blockbuster and Kodak cases,
- build a version 1 eighteen-month transformation roadmap with all seven required elements,
- design an AI governance plan with named tiers, mechanisms, and owners,
- assign section ownership for the final project report and lock a sprint plan.

## Weekend 3 in the semester arc

Teams arrive from the midterm sprint with a completed 25 to 30 slide executive presentation and supporting business case document. Weekend 3 opens with graded midterm presentations in boardroom format. The afternoon and Sunday session shift entirely to final project preparation: operating model design, data strategy, transformation roadmap, AI governance, and section ownership for the final report.
