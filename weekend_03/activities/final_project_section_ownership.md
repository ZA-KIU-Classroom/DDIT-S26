## Instructions

### Step 1. Assign all 10 sections (5 minutes)

Each section gets one primary owner and one reviewer. The owner writes the first draft. The reviewer critiques and improves it before the team review meeting.

No section may have zero owners.

### Step 2. Set internal deadlines (5 minutes)

Work backwards from June 4 submission deadline.
Allow 48 hours for team integration.
Allow 24 hours for final polish.
First drafts must be complete by May 31.

### Step 3. Identify the riskiest section (5 minutes)

Which section is most likely to be weak or incomplete at submission?
Name it.
Assign a second reviewer.
Schedule an explicit team review for that section by May 30.

---

## Quality check

Before submitting:
- Every section has a named owner and a named reviewer.
- No two sections have the same owner unless the team is small enough to justify it.
- The riskiest section has a review date before May 30.
- Internal deadlines are confirmed with the assigned owner verbally before leaving today.
