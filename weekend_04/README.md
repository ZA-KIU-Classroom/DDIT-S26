# Weekend 4 Module

## Dates
Saturday 30 May 2026 — Google Meet (online)
Sunday 31 May 2026 — On campus (in person)
10:00 to 17:00 both days

## Theme
Responsible AI, governance, and final integration

## Syllabus weeks covered
Weeks 13 to 15

## Assessment linkage
This weekend carries the 40-point final exam. Sunday 31 May is the graded boardroom
presentation day. Everything on Saturday exists to maximise the quality of Sunday.

## Learning objectives

By the end of Weekend 4, students should be able to:
- explain the core principles of responsible AI: bias, fairness, transparency, and accountability,
- identify how the EU AI Act and Georgian data protection law apply to their client's AI use cases,
- articulate a governance framework that includes an oversight body, review cadence, and human override protocol,
- describe how human-AI collaboration models change workforce structure and leadership responsibilities,
- deliver a 30-minute boardroom-standard executive presentation with full Q&A under exam conditions,
- submit a comprehensive final report that synthesises all work from Weekends 1 through 4.

## Saturday purpose

Saturday is a teaching and coaching day delivered online via Google Meet. Two
teaching blocks cover the remaining syllabus content: responsible AI and governance
in the morning, future of work and course synthesis after lunch. The afternoon is a
structured coaching session where each team presents their executive summary and
receives targeted feedback before Sunday.

Saturday is not a drafting day. Teams must arrive with a complete presentation deck
and a near-complete report. Saturday coaching refines; it does not construct.

Google Meet link and technical instructions will be sent separately. Join from a
quiet location with a stable connection. Camera on for the full session. Screen
sharing capability must be tested before Saturday 10:00.

## Sunday purpose

Sunday is the final exam. It is held on campus in person. Three boardroom
presentations back to back. Each team presents for 30 minutes and defends for 15
minutes. Peer scoring counts. All final documents must be in the GitHub repository
by 17:00.
