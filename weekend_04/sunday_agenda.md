## 14:30 to 15:30 — Course Synthesis and Close

### Key course takeaways
Five frameworks. Five strategic insights. One sentence each. Students write their
own version before the instructor shares the course version.

1. Rogers' five domains: digital disruption does not attack products, it attacks the relationships between a business and its customers, competitors, data, partners, and innovation ecosystem.
2. Christensen: the most dangerous competitor is not the one attacking your best customers but the one serving the customers you have chosen to ignore.
3. Agrawal, Gans, Goldfarb: when prediction becomes cheap, the value of judgment rises. AI does not replace strategists; it raises the bar for what good judgment requires.
4. Parker, Van Alstyne, Choudary: platform businesses do not own the means of production; they own the means of connection. The most defensible competitive position is not the best product but the largest network.
5. Iansiti and Lakhani: AI-first operating models are not faster versions of traditional operating models. They require a different theory of the firm, a different governance structure, and a different definition of competitive advantage.
