## Deliverable 2: Comprehensive Final Report

**What it is**
The written consulting report that synthesises all work from Weekends 1 through 4.
This is the permanent record of the team's analysis. It contains everything that
does not fit in slides: full methodology, detailed evidence, complete financial
model with assumptions, and the governance framework in full.

**Submission file**
`final_report.pdf`

**Required sections**

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Executive summary | The three most important conclusions. The recommendation in one sentence. The ROI headline. Written last, tested first. Maximum two pages. |
| Industry and competitive analysis | Rogers five domains with evidence. Christensen disruption classification with rationale. Porter Five Forces with named competitors and quantified forces where possible. Key competitive dynamics and their strategic consequences. |
| AI landscape and opportunity analysis | AI adoption evidence by use case. AI Opportunity Map with full scoring rationale. Strategic implications of AI trajectory. Gap analysis against the most AI-advanced comparable organisation. |
| Platform strategy analysis | Platform competitive structure. Network effect assessment. Client's current platform position. Platform threat assessment with named entrants. |
| Strategic options with full evaluation matrix | Three structurally distinct options with full descriptions. Four-criterion evaluation matrix with scores and rationale. Recommended option with three supporting reasons tied to specific findings. |
| Recommended business model | Value proposition in full. Operating model changes by function. Revenue model logic. Data and AI infrastructure requirements. |
| 18-month transformation roadmap | All initiatives with all seven elements per initiative. Horizon tags, owner functions, budget estimates, timelines, dependencies, success metrics, and risks. Sequencing rationale. |
| ROI model with full assumptions log | Investment breakdown by year. Benefit realisation by year. Base case, upside, and downside scenarios with differentiated assumptions. Payback period, IRR, and 5-year NPV. Sensitivity table for the two most consequential assumptions. All non-sourced numbers labelled as assumptions with stated basis. |
| Risk register | All material risks with likelihood, impact, and mitigation. |
| Change management plan | Change management as a named workstream. Budget: 20 to 30 percent of total transformation cost. Owner function. Timeline aligned to the roadmap. Key activities by phase. |
| Governance plan | Oversight body: name, membership, decision rights, review cadence. Human override protocol per high-risk AI use case: trigger threshold, override authority, documentation requirement, maximum review time. EU AI Act risk classification for each AI use case. Georgian data protection law obligations. |
| AI tool disclosure appendix | If AI tools were used substantially in research or writing, disclose which tools were used, for which sections, and how outputs were verified. Required per syllabus AI tool policy. |

**Length**
No minimum. No maximum. The report should be as long as the analysis requires
and no longer. Padding is penalised. Incompleteness is penalised more.

## Deliverable 3: 18-Month Transformation Roadmap

**What it is**
The complete roadmap as a standalone document. This is also included as a section
of the final report but submitted separately so it can be read and assessed
independently.

**Submission file**
`transformation_roadmap_final.pdf` or `transformation_roadmap_final.xlsx`

**Required elements per initiative**

Every initiative in the roadmap must contain all seven of the following. An
initiative missing any element is incomplete.

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Horizon tag | H1 (0 to 12 months), H2 (12 to 24 months), or H3 (24 months plus) |
| Owner function | Named role or team. Not a person's name. |
| Budget estimate | Order of magnitude figure in GEL, tied to the ROI model. |
| Timeline | Start month and end month within the 18-month window. |
| Dependency | The named prerequisite initiative or capability that must be in place before this initiative begins. |
| Success metric | One measurable metric achievable within the initiative timeline. |
| Risk | The primary execution risk for this initiative. |

**Sequencing rules**
- Data governance framework: must appear in months 1 to 3. No exceptions.
- Oversight body establishment: must appear in months 2 to 4.
- Human override protocols documented and tested: must precede any high-risk AI use case go-live.
- Change management workstream: must run in parallel with all H1 and H2 initiatives, not after them.
- First quarterly governance review: must appear at month 6.

## Deliverable 4: Change Management and Governance Plan

**What it is**
The governance and change management framework as a standalone document. This is
also included as a section of the final report but submitted separately.

**Submission file**
`governance_change_management_plan.pdf`

**Change management section must include**
- Change management defined as a named workstream, not a risk mitigation.
- Budget: 20 to 30 percent of total transformation cost. Stated as a GEL figure.
- Owner function: the role or team responsible for leading change management.
- Timeline: a phased activity plan aligned to the roadmap. What happens in each phase.
- Key activities: stakeholder analysis, communication plan, training and capability building, adoption measurement.

**Governance section must include**
- Oversight body: name, membership by function, decision rights, review cadence.
- Human override protocol for every high-risk AI use case:
  - Specific trigger threshold (not "complex cases")
  - Named override authority by role
  - Documentation and audit requirement
  - Maximum review time before default action
- EU AI Act risk tier classification for each AI use case with rationale.
- Georgian data protection law obligations for use cases involving individual automated decisions.
- Escalation path: what happens when the oversight body cannot reach a decision.

## Assessment linkage

All four deliverables are assessed together as the 40-point final exam. They are
not assessed separately. The presentation is the primary instrument. The written
documents provide the analytical depth that cannot be conveyed in 30 minutes.

A strong presentation with weak documentation loses points on strategic insight
and analytical rigor. Strong documentation with a weak presentation loses points
on executive communication and professional standards. Both must meet the standard.

| Criterion | Weight | Primary instrument |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic insight and analytical rigor | 30% | Final report and roadmap |
| Quality and feasibility of transformation roadmap | 30% | Roadmap and governance plan |
| Executive communication and storytelling | 25% | Presentation deck |
| Professional presentation standards and polish | 15% | Live presentation |
