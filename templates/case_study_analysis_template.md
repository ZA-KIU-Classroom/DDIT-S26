# Case Study Analysis Template

## Purpose

Structure your individual case study analysis. This template maps the six required sections and the framework application requirements. The final submission is 10 to 12 pages.

## Instructions

Use this template to outline first. Draft section by section. The executive summary is written last.

## Section 02. Organisation and context (1 page)

Cover enough context for the reader to evaluate the strategic choices. This is not a company history.

Required:
- Industry structure: who the main players are, how value is created, where profit concentrates.
- The organisation's competitive position before the transformation decision.
- The specific strategic challenge the transformation was designed to address.
- One or two numbers that define the stakes.

Condition of change: "If [trigger] occurred, we would instead recommend [alternative] because [reason]."

Apply the Blockbuster constraint analysis:
- What was the board-level constraint? Name the equivalent of late fee revenue.
- How would your recommendation have addressed that constraint specifically?

Apply Three Horizons logic:
- What would the H1, H2, and H3 portfolio have looked like under your recommendation?
- How would H1 ROI have funded H2 infrastructure?

## Quality check

Before submitting:
- Executive summary contains three conclusions with numbers, named entities, and strategic consequences.
- Each of the four frameworks has a specific answerable question and a named insight.
- The strategic assessment names specific evidence for each criterion, not general impressions.
- The alternative recommendation follows Pyramid Principle structure with a condition of change.
- The Blockbuster constraint analysis names the specific business model constraint.
- All three lessons are actionable principles, not observations.
- At least five sources with clear attribution.
